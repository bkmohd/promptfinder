Privacy & disclosure · 17 Oct 2026

Plain language, no dark patterns.

This page explains what the site stores, what third parties may see, and how advertising and affiliate links are handled. It is written for humans and is not legal advice.

What we collect

The site is static. Browsing the library and using the Studio does not require an account, and prompts you type or paste into the Studio or the Prompt Doctor are processed in your browser — they are not uploaded to us.

If you subscribe to the newsletter, we store the email address you submit with our email provider. If you email us, we keep the correspondence. That is the extent of the personal data we intentionally collect.

What is stored on your device

  • Local storage: a record that you subscribed (so the form doesn't repeat) and small interface preferences. You can clear it in your browser settings at any time.
  • No tracking pixels from us. We do not operate our own cross-site tracking.

Analytics

We aim to use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics (for example Plausible or a comparable cookieless tool) to understand which pages and prompts are used. If cookie-based analytics are enabled, this page and the site's consent banner will say so before any non-essential cookie is set.

Advertising

This site is ad-supported. If Google AdSense or another ad network is active, third-party vendors — including Google — may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

Where required (for example in the EEA or UK), a consent banner is shown before non-essential ads or cookies load. Advertising is never placed inside the Studio output or between a prompt and its copy button.

Affiliate links

Some links to tools or model providers may be affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission if you subscribe. Affiliate relationships never influence a prompt's test score, ranking or inclusion. Sponsored content is labelled as sponsored.

Third parties

We link to model providers and other websites. Their privacy practices are their own. Newsletter delivery is handled by an email service provider acting as our processor; unsubscribe links are included in every email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port personal data we hold about you, and to object to processing. To make a request, email privacy@promptfoundry.example. We respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, note the change in the site changelog.
