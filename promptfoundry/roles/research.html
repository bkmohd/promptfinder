Questions

The honest answers.

How were these researchers prompts tested?

Each prompt was run ten times per model with identical inputs, then scored against a 40-point rubric: works without edits (15), follows the requested format (10), handles facts safely (10) and sounds like the brief (5). Pass rates shown on each card come from the last completed cycle (17 Oct 2026). Failures stay visible.

Which AI model should a researcher use?

Start with the models listed on the prompt itself. In our current cycle, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 score highest on structured documents, Gemini 3.8 on research with citations, Grok 4.7 when current information matters, and Midjourney V8.2 or GPT Image 2.5 for visuals. Every prompt page lists the models it was actually run on.

Can I use these prompts at work?

Yes. Prompts are free to copy, adapt and use commercially. You may not repackage the library wholesale or resell it as a prompt pack. Attribution is welcome but not required.
